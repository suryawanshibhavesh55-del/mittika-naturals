Mindful Skincare Ritual

How to Use

Enjoy a calm, refreshing face ritual with pure earth clays. Follow our packaging-approved steps to mix, apply, and nourish your skin.

Official Packaging Instructions

The Four-Step Application Guide

Crafted for simple, effortless application at home.

1

Mix the Powder

Mix 1–2 teaspoons of powder with water or rose water to form a smooth paste.

2

Apply Evenly

Apply evenly on clean, dry face, gently covering facial skin while avoiding the eye area.

3

Relax 8–10 Min

Leave for 8–10 minutes, then rinse off thoroughly with clean water.

4

Pat & Moisturize

Pat dry and follow with a light moisturizer or aloe vera gel.

Official Mittika Naturals Ritual Guide for Oily, Dry, and Normal Skin

Tailored For Your Skin

Ritual by Skin Type

Customize your mask mixer for optimal balance:

For Oily Skin

Take 1 tbsp of face mask powder and make paste by adding enough rose water. Apply this paste all over your face and neck. Leave it for 20 minutes and wash it off with cold water.

For Dry Skin

Mix 1 tbsp of face mask powder with a little milk cream & honey. Apply this paste all over face and neck. Leave it for 10–15 minutes and wash it off with lukewarm water. Apply your favourite moisturizer.

For Normal Skin

Mix 1 teaspoon of the face mask with rose water or normal water and apply evenly on the face and neck. Leave for 10–15 minutes, then rinse thoroughly with water.

Safety & Storage Guidelines
  • For external use only
  • Patch test recommended before first use
  • Avoid contact with eyes
  • Store in a cool, dry place at room temperature
  • Keep away from direct sunlight
