Our Heritage & Ethos

About Mittika Naturals

Rooted in the timeless simplicity of earth-derived clays and botanical ingredients for thoughtful, modern beauty rituals.

“Mittika Naturals was created with a simple idea — natural skincare should feel simple, honest and connected to nature.”
The Mittika Naturals Founding Principle

Inspired by the timeless use of earth-derived clays and botanical ingredients in traditional beauty rituals, Mittika Naturals brings these ingredients into a modern skincare experience.

Our journey began with a desire to create skincare products that are easy to understand, thoughtfully formulated and rooted in the goodness of nature.

Our first hero product, the 4 Clay Detox Face Mask Powder, brings together four distinctive clays — Multani Mitti, Kaolin Clay, French Green Clay and Rose Clay — along with a carefully selected blend of botanical ingredients.

We believe good skincare is not about making complicated promises. It is about choosing thoughtful ingredients, creating simple rituals and being transparent about what goes into every product.
