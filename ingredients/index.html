The Core Mineral Matrix

4 Clay Ingredients

Four distinct clays working in synergy — each offering unique mineral density, oil-adsorption capability, and gentle skin-refining texture.

Multani Mitti

A celebrated traditional mineral clay known for absorbing excess oil, gently purifying facial pores, and leaving skin feeling clean, clear, and balanced.

02

Kaolin Clay

An ultra-gentle, mild mineral clay that softens the mask texture, suitable for delicate skin, providing smooth application without over-drying.

03

French Green Clay

Naturally rich in essential earth minerals and decomposed plant material, renowned for deep clarifying action and refreshing tired, congested skin.

Rose Clay

A delicate naturally pink clay that offers gentle exfoliation, helps improve skin texture, and promotes a naturally radiant, refreshed complexion.
