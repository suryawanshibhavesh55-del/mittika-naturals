Product & Cosmetic Disclaimer

Please read these essential usage notes and guidelines regarding natural ingredients, formulation properties, and personal skin care.

1. Cosmetic Nature of Products

The products offered by Mittika Naturals, specifically the 4 Clay Detox Face Mask Powder – 100 g, are cosmetic skincare formulations designed for personal topical grooming, cleansing, and refreshing rituals.

Our products and descriptions are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical disease, clinical condition, or severe dermatological disorder (such as cystic acne, dermatitis, or eczema). If you are undergoing medical treatment or have chronic skin conditions, please consult your healthcare professional or dermatologist prior to introducing new skincare products.

2. Natural Ingredients & Batch Variations

Our mask powder is formulated with natural earth-mined clays (Multani Mitti, Kaolin Clay, French Green Clay, Rose Clay) and pure botanical herbs (Neem, Tulsi, Turmeric, Orange Peel, Mulethi, Sandalwood, Rose Petal, Colloidal Oatmeal, Aloe Vera, Cucumber).

Because these are natural earth and agricultural ingredients, slight variations in natural shade, fine texture, and earthy aroma may occur naturally between seasonal harvests and production batches. These variations reflect the authenticity of unadulterated natural ingredients and do not affect product quality.

3. Mandatory Patch Test Recommendation

Even the purest natural clays and botanical herbs may cause individual sensitivities in select individuals. In accordance with standard cosmetic best practices:

  • Always conduct a small patch test on your inner forearm before first facial application.
  • Mix a pinch of powder with clean water, apply to a coin-sized area, and leave for 10 minutes before rinsing.
  • Monitor the area for 24 hours. If any irritation, unusual redness, or discomfort occurs, discontinue use immediately.

4. Safety Precautions & Storage Guidelines

As stated on our certified product packaging:

  • For external use only.
  • Avoid contact with eyes. In case of accidental contact, rinse thoroughly with cool water.
  • Do not ingest or inhale dry powder.
  • Storage: Store the sealed pouch in a cool, dry place at room temperature. Keep away from humidity, moisture ingress, and direct sunlight.
  • Keep out of reach of young children.

5. Brand Commitment to Honest Representation

Mittika Naturals does not make inflated, therapeutic, or guaranteed-result promises. Individual skin responses vary based on lifestyle, climate, diet, and unique skin types. Our goal is to offer a transparent, thoughtfully crafted, and grounded natural clay ritual that leaves your skin feeling refreshed, clean, and balanced.
