Follow our simple 4-step ritual:
1. Mix 1–2 teaspoons of powder with water or rose water to form a smooth paste.
2. Apply evenly on a clean, dry face.
3. Leave for 8–10 minutes, then rinse off thoroughly with water.
4. Pat dry and follow with a light moisturizer or aloe vera gel.

Generally 8–10 minutes is ideal. For oily skin, you may leave it on for up to 20 minutes before rinsing with cold water. For dry or normal skin, 10–15 minutes is recommended. Never allow a clay mask to dry to the point of cracking or extreme tightness.
