Step-By-Step

Official How-To-Use Instructions

1

Mix Smoothly

Mix 1–2 teaspoons of powder with water or rose water to form a smooth paste.

2

Apply Evenly

Apply evenly on clean, dry face, avoiding delicate eye contours and lips.

3

Rest 8–10 Min

Leave for 8–10 minutes, then rinse off thoroughly with clean water.

4

Moisturize

Pat dry and follow with a light moisturizer or aloe vera gel.

Safety & Storage Guidelines
  • For external use only
  • Patch test recommended before first use
  • Avoid contact with eyes
  • Store in a cool, dry place at room temperature
  • Keep away from direct sunlight
